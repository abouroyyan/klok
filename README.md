A clock that behaves like a real clock.
Most clock apps try to do everything. Alarms, weather, calendars, widgets, reminders, productivity tools, and countless settings.
But Klok does none of that.

It was born from a simple frustration while staying in hotels and unfamiliar places. I realized how often I wanted to know the time without reaching for my phone, unlocking it, or getting distracted by notifications.

When a clock sits on a wall or a bedside table, you simply glance at it.
That's the experience Klok tries to bring back. Open the app, place your phone on a desk, shelf, or bedside table, and the time is always there—large, clear, and visible from across the room.
